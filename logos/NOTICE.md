# Logo art attribution

The default ASCII artworks in this directory come from
[fastfetch](https://github.com/fastfetch-cli/fastfetch), revision
`bbb92543b0f8` (2026-10-17), and are used under the MIT licence in
`LICENSE-fastfetch`. The marks depict distribution identities and remain
the property of their respective projects; they are used here
descriptively to identify the running system.

Upstream logo files are named after their distro id; the compact variants
end in `-small`.
